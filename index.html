Glass, Mirror & Aluminum Services Across Kuwait

Design Glass & Mirror Kuwait specializes in custom glass, mirror and aluminum solutions for residential, commercial and architectural projects. We provide modern glass products and installation services designed around the requirements of each project.

Our glass services include tempered glass, shower glass, glass shower doors, sliding glass doors, hinged glass doors, staircase glass, glass balustrades, balcony glass, pool glass, swimming pool glass, architectural glass, shopfront glass, office glass partitions and glass windows.

We also provide aluminum doors, aluminum sliding doors, aluminum windows and aluminum glass systems. For interior spaces, our mirror services include custom mirrors, decorative mirrors, bathroom mirrors, LED mirrors and backlit designer mirrors.
